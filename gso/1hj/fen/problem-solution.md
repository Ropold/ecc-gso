# Problem-Solution Examples

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| The video quality is very poor during meetings. | Check your internet connection and move closer to your router. Turn off your camera temporarily. Close other programs and browser tabs. Ask others to turn off their cameras. |
| I can't hear other participants in the conference. | Check if your speakers are muted in the app. Increase your computer volume. Try using headphones. Restart the application. Check your audio output settings. |
| The screen sharing is not working. | Give the app permission to share your screen in system settings. Restart the application. Share a single window instead of your entire screen. Update the app to the latest version. |
