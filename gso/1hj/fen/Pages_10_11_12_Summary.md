# Business Complaints - Problems and Solutions Summary

## Pages 10, 11, 12 - Making and Dealing with Complaints

| # | Problem | Solution |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Delayed or missing delivery/service. | Contact supplier politely by phone or email to inform about the problem. |
| 2 | Incorrect delivery (wrong quantity/size/quality received). | State the specific issue and what was ordered versus what was received. |
| 3 | Wrong model sent instead of ordered item. | Write with reference to order number and inform about the mistake. |
| 4 | Damaged items received in delivery. | Inform sender, offer to send pictures, and request replacement by specific deadline. |
| 5 | Missing items in shipment. | Request immediate dispatch of the missing items. |
| 6 | Invoice errors or mixed up numbers. | Call to clarify the invoice discrepancies with the accounting department. |
| 7 | Missing items discovered during unpacking. | Inform supplier about exact number of missing items from goods control. |
| 8 | Damaged, defective, or broken items. | Point out the damage and request replacement or proper repair. |
| 9 | Late delivery (already overdue by specified time). | Mention expected delivery date and request status update on shipment. |
| 10 | Poor repair work execution or quality. | Point out the poor quality work and request proper execution. |
| 11 | Damage appears to be from rough handling in transit. | Explain suspected cause and request replacement shipment. |
| 12 | Order mixed up with another customer's order. | Clarify the mix-up and request correct order to be sent. |
| 13 | Faulty or defective goods received. | Request replacement of faulty goods at supplier's expense. |
| 14 | Defective articles need to be returned. | Request supplier to arrange collection of defective items from warehouse. |
| 15 | Quality issues warrant compensation. | Request price reduction (e.g., 20%) or price cut to specific amount. |
| 16 | Customer complaint received about your service/product. | Stay calm and polite; thank customer for bringing attention to issue. |
| 17 | Need to explain what happened to customer. | Provide detailed explanation and involve customer in finding the solution. |
| 18 | Cannot provide immediate explanation for issue. | Apologize sincerely and promise compensation or proposed corrective measures. |
| 19 | Complaint received is not justified or not your fault. | Politely reject complaint with supporting evidence if clearly not at fault. |
| 20 | Cultural differences in complaint communication style. | Use conciliatory tone and indirect language in English business communication. |
